# Snappy

> Snappy is a corporate gifting platform that lets businesses send curated gifts, branded swag, and recipient-choice collections to employees and customers. Beyond its web app, Snappy publishes a developer API suite that embeds gifting into CRMs, HRIS platforms, and marketing-automation tools, triggering gifts from business events (recognition, loyalty, sales milestones) with budget controls and approval workflows.
